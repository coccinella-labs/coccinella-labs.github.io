harper

An agent harness, not a pretty chatbot. Harper plans real work, executes it as sandboxed jobs, pauses for your approval, and ships audited results, end to end.

  • Deploy in days, not quarters

    Drop-in products with clear integration points, tailored for your stack.

  • Perpetual license or SaaS

    Buy the software outright or run it managed. Your data stays yours either way.

  • Self-hosted, even air-gapped

    Single binaries with no telemetry. Works where your network ends.

  • Direct line to maintainers

    You talk to the people who wrote it, not a ticket queue.
